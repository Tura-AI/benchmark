02 / Knife work

Prep the squid

Pull

Separate head and innards from the mantle in one steady motion.

Trim

Cut below the eyes; keep tentacles, discard beak and innards.

Clean

Remove quill and rinse the cavity. Peel skin only for a pale finish.

Dry

Blot thoroughly. Surface water defeats browning and crisp coatings.

03 / Texture logic

Heat is the choice

Squid is tender at the edges of time: cook it very fast, or let it relax slowly.

Japan · festival & izakaya tradition

Ikayaki

Soy-glazed grilled whole squid

2010 prep · 10 cook

Ingredients · serves 4

  • 500 g cleaned whole squid
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp sake
  • 2 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • ½ lemon, to serve

Prepare & cook

  1. Score · 5 min. Dry squid; cut shallow lines across mantle without slicing through.
  2. Glaze · 2 min. Mix soy, mirin, sake and ginger; brush over squid.
  3. Grill · 4 min. Oil a very hot grill; cook 2 minutes per side.
  4. Lacquer · 4–6 min. Brush and turn twice more, until lightly charred. Slice and serve with lemon.

Korea · everyday home cooking

Ojingeo-bokkeum

Spicy wok-fried squid and vegetables

2214 prep · 8 cook

Ingredients · serves 3

  • 450 g cleaned squid
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 3 scallions, cut
  • 1 green chile
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp rice syrup
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp sesame oil

Prepare & cook

  1. Cut · 8 min. Score mantle, then cut squid and vegetables into bite-size pieces.
  2. Mix · 4 min. Combine soy, chile flakes, paste, syrup, garlic and sesame oil.
  3. Sear · 4 min. Stir-fry vegetables in a hot oiled pan until barely tender.
  4. Flash · 3–4 min. Add squid and sauce; toss hard just until opaque and curled.

Cantonese Chinese · dai pai dong tradition

Salt & pepper squid

Crisp-coated squid with fried aromatics

3525 prep · 10 cook

Ingredients · serves 4

  • 450 g squid, rings
  • 80 ml whole milk
  • 65 g plain flour
  • 65 g cornstarch
  • 1 tsp sea salt
  • ¾ tsp white pepper
  • 750 ml frying oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 green chile
  • 2 scallions

Prepare & cook

  1. Soak · 20 min. Toss dry squid with milk; meanwhile combine flour, starch, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat · 3 min. Drain well and press squid into the dry mixture.
  3. Fry · 4 min. At 190°C, fry in small batches for 60–90 seconds; drain.
  4. Finish · 2 min. Fry garlic, chile and scallion briefly; toss with squid and season.

Taiwan · san bei tradition

Three-cup squid

Rice wine, soy and sesame reduction

2012 prep · 8 cook

Ingredients · serves 3

  • 1 large squid
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp rice wine
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 10–12 ginger slices
  • 1 red chile
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 handful Thai basil

Prepare & cook

  1. Blanch · 1 min. Score and cut squid; blanch 30 seconds and drain dry.
  2. Bloom · 3 min. Warm sesame oil; fry ginger, garlic and chile until fragrant.
  3. Reduce · 3 min. Add squid, soy, wine and mirin; toss over high heat until glossy.
  4. Perfume · 1 min. Fold in scallions and basil; cover 30 seconds, then serve.

Cantonese Chinese · home-style steaming

Steamed garlic squid

Squid and glass noodles with ginger sauce

2315 prep · 8 cook

Ingredients · serves 3

  • 500 g squid
  • 60 g glass noodles
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp minced ginger
  • 2 tbsp light soy
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 scallions

Prepare & cook

  1. Soak · 10 min. Soften noodles in warm water; drain and spread on a heatproof plate.
  2. Arrange · 3 min. Score squid, cut into pieces and set over noodles.
  3. Sauce · 2 min. Mix garlic, ginger, soy, oyster sauce, sugar and sesame oil; spoon over.
  4. Steam · 6–8 min. Steam over rapidly boiling water until squid just turns opaque. Add scallion.

Japan · nimono tradition

Ika daikon

Squid and radish in seasoned dashi

4515 prep · 30 cook

Ingredients · serves 2

  • 100 g squid
  • 400 g daikon
  • 20 g ginger
  • 300 ml dashi
  • 50 ml sake
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • to taste yuzu peel

Prepare & cook

  1. Cut · 10 min. Peel daikon into 2 cm half-moons; score squid and cut into strips.
  2. Brown · 4 min. Sauté daikon in oil until its edges turn translucent.
  3. Simmer · 20 min. Add dashi, sake, soy, sugar and ginger; cover and cook daikon gently.
  4. Finish · 4–5 min. Add squid only at the end. Rest 5 minutes; garnish with yuzu.

Korea · muchim side-dish tradition

Ojingeo muchim

Blanched squid in a sweet-sour chile dressing

2520 prep · 5 cook

Ingredients · serves 4

  • 1 large squid, 350 g
  • 1 cucumber
  • ¼ onion
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 green chile
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1½ tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp corn syrup
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Prepare & cook

  1. Salt · 10 min. Slice cucumber; salt lightly, rest, then squeeze dry. Slice aromatics.
  2. Blanch · 1 min. Lower scored squid into boiling water for 45–60 seconds; chill and drain.
  3. Dress · 3 min. Whisk gochujang, vinegar, sugar, syrup and sesame oil.
  4. Toss · 2 min. Combine squid and vegetables just before serving; scatter sesame.

Korea · buchimgae tradition

Squid buchimgae

Crisp savory pancake with vegetables

3015 prep · 15 cook

Ingredients · makes 6

  • 1 small squid tube
  • ½ onion
  • ½ carrot
  • ½ zucchini
  • 50 g oyster mushrooms
  • 125 g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 180 ml ice water
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil

Prepare & cook

  1. Slice · 10 min. Cut squid and vegetables into thin, short strips for even cooking.
  2. Batter · 3 min. Whisk flour, egg, ice water and salt only until combined; fold in fillings.
  3. Griddle · 10 min. Spoon six pancakes into a generously oiled pan; press thin.
  4. Flip · 5 min. Cook each side until deeply golden and crisp. Drain and serve hot.

Hokkaido, Japan · regional cuisine

Ikameshi

Glutinous rice–stuffed braised squid

9535 prep · 60 cook

Ingredients · serves 4

  • 4 small whole squid
  • 200 g glutinous rice
  • 600 ml dashi
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • to serve red ginger

Prepare & cook

  1. Soak · 30 min. Wash glutinous rice, soak, then drain thoroughly.
  2. Stuff · 5 min. Fill cleaned mantles only halfway; secure openings with toothpicks.
  3. Braise · 45 min. Cover with dashi, sake, soy, sugar and salt; simmer gently, turning.
  4. Glaze · 15 min. Uncover and reduce until glossy. Rest, slice and add red ginger.

Taiwan · street-food soup tradition

Squid soup

Sweet-sour broth built from fried aromatics

2510 prep · 15 cook

Ingredients · serves 2

  • 2 squid tubes
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup sliced carrot
  • 5 slices ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 scallion
  • 120 ml hot water
  • 1 tbsp light soy
  • 1 tsp black vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg, optional

Prepare & cook

  1. Cut · 8 min. Score squid into diamonds; slice carrot, ginger, garlic and scallion.
  2. Fry · 4 min. Sauté carrot and aromatics over medium-high heat until fragrant.
  3. Build · 6 min. Add squid, then hot water, soy, vinegar and sugar; simmer briefly.
  4. Ribbon · 2 min. Stream in beaten egg, if using. Stop as soon as squid is opaque.

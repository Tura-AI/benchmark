Ikayaki
Grilled Whole Squid

Prep 12 minCook 6–8 minHigh heat

Ingredients

  • 2 cleaned whole squid, 250 g each
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tbsp sake
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp grated ginger

Method

  1. Score bodies every 1 cm; thread or skewer flat.
  2. Simmer soy, mirin, sake, sugar and ginger 2 minutes.
  3. Grill squid over very high heat 2 minutes per side.
  4. Brush repeatedly with tare; grill 1–2 minutes more until lacquered.

Soy lacquer · smoke · springy bite

Ojingeo-bokkeum
Spicy Stir-fried Squid

Prep 20 minCook 5 minWok / skillet

Ingredients

  • 500 g cleaned squid, sliced
  • 1 small onion, 1 carrot
  • 2 scallions
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp rice syrup
  • 2 tsp sesame oil, 2 garlic cloves

Method

  1. Mix chile paste, flakes, soy, syrup, sesame oil and garlic.
  2. Heat pan until smoking; stir-fry vegetables 2 minutes.
  3. Add squid and sauce; toss hard 2–3 minutes only.
  4. Finish with scallion and sesame seeds; serve immediately.

Gochujang · sesame · fierce wok heat

Jiu Yim Sin Yau
Salt & Pepper Squid

Prep 25 minFry 4 min190°C oil

Ingredients

  • 500 g squid tubes, scored
  • 60 g potato starch
  • ½ tsp white pepper, ¾ tsp salt
  • 500 ml frying oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 red chile, 2 scallions

Method

  1. Dry squid thoroughly; toss with starch, salt and pepper.
  2. Fry in batches 60–90 seconds until crisp and curled.
  3. In a clean wok, sizzle garlic, chile and scallion 20 seconds.
  4. Toss fried squid through aromatics; season and plate.
Recipe sourceYouTube cooking video
Feathery crust · garlic · white pepper
Taiwan · Sanbeiji clay-pot tradition

Sanbei Youyu
Three-cup Squid

Prep 15 minCook 12 minClay pot / wok

Ingredients

  • 500 g squid, bite-size pieces
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice wine
  • 8 ginger slices, 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 red chile, 1 packed cup Thai basil
  • 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Warm sesame oil; fry ginger and garlic until golden.
  2. Add squid and chile; sear 90 seconds.
  3. Add soy, rice wine and sugar; bubble 3–4 minutes.
  4. Reduce until glossy, then fold in basil off heat.
Technique sourceYouTube cooking video

This squid adaptation uses the classic Taiwanese “three cup” flavor architecture.

Ika Geso Karaage
Fried Squid Tentacles

Marinate 15 minFry 3 min175°C oil

Ingredients

  • 400 g squid tentacles
  • 1½ tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sake
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 70 g potato starch
  • Oil, lemon, shichimi

Method

  1. Cut tentacles into clusters; marinate with soy, sake, ginger and garlic.
  2. Drain well and coat generously in potato starch.
  3. Fry in small batches 2–3 minutes until craggy and crisp.
  4. Drain, dust with shichimi and serve with lemon.

Potato starch · ginger · izakaya crunch

Ojingeo-sundae
Stuffed Steamed Squid

Prep 30 minSteam 18 minRest 8 min

Ingredients

  • 2 whole squid, cleaned
  • 80 g cooked glass noodles, chopped
  • 120 g firm tofu, squeezed
  • 80 g minced pork or mushrooms
  • ½ carrot, 2 scallions
  • 1 egg, 1 tsp sesame oil

Method

  1. Mix noodles, tofu, pork, vegetables, egg and sesame oil.
  2. Fill squid bodies only two-thirds full; secure openings.
  3. Steam over medium heat 15–18 minutes.
  4. Rest before slicing into thick rounds; serve with soy-vinegar.
Recipe sourceYouTube cooking video
Sokcho specialty · savory noodle filling
Hong Kong · Cantonese home-style steaming

Jing Sin Yau
Steamed Garlic Squid

Prep 15 minSteam 4–5 minFinish with hot oil

Ingredients

  • 500 g cleaned squid, scored
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 scallions, julienned
  • 1½ tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. Arrange squid in one layer; scatter garlic and ginger.
  2. Steam over vigorously boiling water 4–5 minutes.
  3. Drain excess liquid; spoon over soy and sesame oil.
  4. Top with scallion and pour over smoking neutral oil.

Gan Guo Youyu
Dry-pot Squid

Prep 25 minCook 10 minWok

Ingredients

  • 500 g squid, scored
  • 150 g lotus root, thinly sliced
  • 2 celery stalks
  • 8 dried chiles
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorn
  • 1½ tbsp doubanjiang
  • 3 garlic cloves, ginger, cilantro

Method

  1. Blanch lotus root 2 minutes; drain. Flash-blanch squid 20 seconds.
  2. Wok-fry chiles and peppercorn in oil until fragrant.
  3. Add doubanjiang, garlic and ginger; fry until red oil blooms.
  4. Add vegetables and squid; toss 2 minutes, finish with cilantro.
Dry-pot technique sourceYouTube cooking video
Doubanjiang · numbing pepper · chile
Okinawa, Japan · Sumiso-ae tradition

Ika no Sumiso-ae
Blanched Squid with Miso

Prep 15 minBlanch 45 secChill 5 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g squid, thin strips
  • 1 small cucumber, salted
  • 40 g rehydrated wakame
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp Japanese mustard

Method

  1. Whisk miso, vinegar, sugar and mustard until smooth.
  2. Blanch squid 30–45 seconds; shock in ice water and dry.
  3. Squeeze cucumber dry; combine with squid and wakame.
  4. Dress just before serving; keep cool, not icy.
Recipe sourceYouTube cooking video
Cool · tangy · mustard-miso brightness
Macau · Macanese Portuguese–Chinese tradition

Caril de Lula
Baked Coconut Curry Squid

Prep 20 minCook 30 minBake 200°C

Ingredients

  • 500 g squid rings
  • 250 g potato, 1.5 cm cubes
  • ½ onion, ½ red pepper
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 100 ml stock
  • 2 garlic cloves, 1 bay leaf

Method

  1. Parboil potato 6 minutes. Sauté onion, pepper and garlic.
  2. Bloom curry powder; add coconut milk, stock and bay.
  3. Simmer potatoes 8 minutes; fold in squid for 1 minute.
  4. Transfer to casserole and bake 10–12 minutes until bubbling.
Recipe sourceYouTube cooking video
Coconut · curry · Lusophone Macau

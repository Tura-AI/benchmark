Method & sources

Historical series

1986–2025 values are estimates at the Paris coordinate (48.8566° N, 2.3522° E) from the ERA5-Land reanalysis served by Open-Meteo. Each seasonal mean averages 92 daily mean 2 m air temperatures from 1 June through 31 August. “Hottest day” is the maximum modeled daily high in that window.

Reanalysis combines observations with a weather model on an approximately 11 km grid. It is spatially consistent, but it is not the same as a thermometer reading at the Paris-Montsouris station.

2026 value

The outlined 2026 column is a provisional trend projection of 20.2°C. It is an ordinary least-squares extension of the 1986–2025 seasonal means, not an observed summer value and not an operational weather forecast. No 2026 hottest-day value is inferred.

Heat events

  • 2003: Santé publique France reports Paris exceeded 35°C on ten days during the August heat wave.
  • 2018: Santé publique France documents the national heat wave from 24 July to 8 August.
  • 2019: the 42.6°C Paris station record is reported separately from the reanalysis series.
  • 2022: Yale Environment 360 documents the August Paris heat wave.
